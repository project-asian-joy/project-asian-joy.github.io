Speaking
Engagements

I share stories that need to be heard
Invite me to inspire your audience and activate community

Recognized as a Ten Outstanding Young Leader by the Greater Boston Chamber of Commerce and honored as a Recent Outstanding Graduate by Boston Latin School, I’ve had the privilege of speaking in rooms that shaped me—from young professionals finding their footing to artists, organizers, and civic leaders gathered at the The Boston Foundation’s Asian Community Fund AAPI Arts & Culture Summit. In every space, I come back to the same core belief: community changes everything.

A lot of what I share comes from lived experience. Resilience has meant choosing to keep showing up, to keep building, and to keep believing in collective possibility even when it would be easier not to. I believe art is how we tell the truth about who we are, how we honor cultural memory, and how we imagine futures that don’t yet exist.

Whether I’m speaking to students, cross-sector leaders, or emerging changemakers, I leave audiences with next steps: build community on purpose. Lead because you care. Remember that the spaces we create for one another often matter more than the platforms we stand on.

If you’re looking for a speaker who will move a room from inspiration to action, I invite you to bring me into your next gathering.

About the
Artist

I am a citizen artist, art instigator, culture seeder
Exploring the power of creativity to connect and energize community

Anita Yip is a citizen artist, cultural strategist, and community builder. She makes work where art, memory, and collective power meet—through photography, writing, public programs and experiences that invite people to engage with history, place, and one another.

Under the moniker Project Asian Joy, Anita brings overlooked stories to life, transforming them into shared spaces for reflection, connection, and action. She sees as civic infrastructure: a way communities remember who they are, imagine who they can become, and invest in the people and spaces around them.

Recognized as a Ten Outstanding Young Leader by the Greater Boston Chamber of Commerce and a Recent Outstanding Graduate of Boston Latin School, Anita also speaks widely about community, resilience, and the power of creativity to activate change. Her work invites audiences not just to witness culture, but to help shape it.
